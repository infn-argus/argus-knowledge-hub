The **Knowledge base** holds what is written: procedures, manuals, design and commissioning reports,
drawings, notes. A document is linked to the equipment, types and other documents it applies to.

## Write a document

1. **+ New → Document**.
2. **Title**, **Type** (procedure, manual, report…) and its **Code** (proposed from the type).
3. **Body**, written in Markdown. Attach files (a PDF, a drawing) if the content is there.
4. **Confidentiality**: *Pubblico*, *Interno*, *Riservato*… A *riservato* document is sent to an AI only
   if the workspace allows it.
5. **Authority level**: whether it is official (*Ufficiale*), informative, or an internal draft.
6. Link it to where it applies (assets, types), and save.

## Revisions and approval

A controlled document changes through **revisions**:

1. Edit it: your change is a new revision, a draft.
2. Submit the revision for approval.
3. **Someone else approves it**: the author of a revision cannot approve it. A rejection needs a reason.
4. The approved revision is **published**; earlier ones stay in its history.

A document replaced by another is **retired** and points to its successor (*Replaced by…*).

## Retention

Each document has a retention class (permanent, 10, 5 or 2 years, or none), counted from its last
publication or its retirement. A released document cannot be deleted before its retention runs out,
only retired.

## Find what is written

- **Knowledge base → Search**, or the search box at the top.
- **Ask ARGUS** searches documents, tickets, comments and the text of attached files (page by page) by
  meaning, in any language.
- **Type suggestions** proposes a type for documents that have none.
