# ARGUS catalogue icons

Drawn for ARGUS where Tabler has nothing fitting, in Tabler's style (24×24, 2px stroke, round caps and
joins, `currentColor`), so they sit with the others:

- `dipole-magnet.svg`: a C-shaped dipole seen along the beam (Tabler's `magnet` is a horseshoe).
- `quadrupole.svg`: a quadrupole's yoke and four poles around the beam.
- `vacuum-cross.svg`: a four-way vacuum cross with its flanges.
- `vacuum-valve.svg`: the piping-diagram valve symbol with its actuator.

They are part of ARGUS and under its terms. The others, in `../tabler/`, are Tabler Icons 3.48.0
(https://tabler.io/icons), MIT licence (`../tabler/LICENSE`), copied unchanged.
